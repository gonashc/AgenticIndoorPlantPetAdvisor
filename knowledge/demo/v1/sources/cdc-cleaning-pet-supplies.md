# Cleaning and disinfecting pet supplies

CDC distinguishes cleaning, which removes dirt and germs with soap or detergent, from disinfecting,
which uses chemicals to kill germs. Product instructions should be followed. The infographic's
general schedule is to clean wet-food bowls after each use, dry-food and water bowls daily, beds,
blankets, and habitats weekly, and toys monthly. More frequent cleaning or an added disinfection
step may be appropriate when a pet is sick, an item contacts urine or feces, or a household member
has a higher risk of serious illness.

Hard items may be cleaned with soapy water; dishwasher-safe items may use a dishwasher. Soft items
may use a washing machine and dryer when their care instructions allow it. Disinfectant labels must
be followed exactly, pets must be kept away during use, chemicals must be stored securely, and an
item should be dry and safe before it is returned to the pet. This is general hygiene information,
not individualized medical advice.
