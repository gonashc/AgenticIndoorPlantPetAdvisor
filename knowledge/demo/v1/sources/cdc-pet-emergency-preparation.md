# Pet emergency preparation

CDC recommends preparing a portable emergency kit before an evacuation is necessary. Important
documents include veterinary and vaccination records, rabies certificates, prescriptions,
ownership or adoption records, a description and recent photograph of each pet, microchip details,
and emergency contact information. Hard copies should be protected in a waterproof container.

The checklist recommends planning for two weeks of food, water, and necessary medication for each
animal, together with feeding and medication instructions, non-spill dishes, and a manual can
opener. Other useful supplies include a leash, identification collar, harness, suitable carrier,
bedding, toys, first-aid references and supplies, cleanup materials, and cat litter supplies when
applicable. The contents should be adapted with a veterinarian for the animal's needs and checked
regularly so food, medicine, documents, and contact information remain current.
