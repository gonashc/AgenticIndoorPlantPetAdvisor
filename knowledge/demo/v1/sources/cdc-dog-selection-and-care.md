# General dog selection and care

CDC guidance recommends researching care needs before acquiring or adopting a dog and discussing
food, care, exercise, and environment with a veterinarian. A household should consider the dog's
attitude, temperament, size, and activity level alongside the family, home, and available time.
This supports asking lifestyle and housing questions, but it does not assign compatibility scores
to a breed or to any of this application's generalized dog profiles.

Children should be supervised around dogs, including familiar family dogs. A newly adopted dog
should receive veterinary attention soon after adoption, and routine veterinary care, appropriate
vaccinations, parasite prevention, clean water, suitable food, a clean environment, exercise, and
responsible socialization remain ongoing responsibilities. Local ownership and housing rules can
vary and must be checked through a current local source rather than this static knowledge record.
