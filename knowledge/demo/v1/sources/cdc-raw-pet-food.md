# Raw pet food considerations

CDC does not recommend feeding raw food or raw treats to dogs or cats. Raw animal protein that has
not been heated sufficiently can carry foodborne germs. A raw product is not automatically healthier
or nutritionally complete; both raw and cooked products can use ingredients of differing quality and
can be balanced or inadequate. Anyone considering a raw main diet should first consult a veterinarian
or veterinary nutritionist, especially when a young animal, an animal with health problems, a young
child, an older adult, a pregnant person, or someone with a weakened immune system may be exposed.

Handling precautions include washing hands, cleaning contacted surfaces and items, keeping raw pet
food sealed and separate from other food, freezing it until needed, thawing it in the refrigerator,
and discarding leftovers left at room temperature. This record supports a safety warning and does
not prescribe a diet or make claims about a specific commercial product.
