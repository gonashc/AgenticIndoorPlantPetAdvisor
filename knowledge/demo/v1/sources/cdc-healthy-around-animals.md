# Healthy habits around animals

Animals can carry germs even when they appear clean and healthy. CDC educational guidance advises
washing hands after playing with, feeding, or cleaning up after a pet and after touching cages,
toys, food, or other supplies. Food and drink should be kept away from animal areas, and pets should
be kept away from places where human food is prepared or eaten.

Children should handle pets gently, ask an adult before touching an unfamiliar animal, and tell an
adult about a scratch or bite. Adults should help children with pet care and handwashing. Pets need
regular veterinary care, exercise, attention, and an environment that supports safe interaction.
This source provides household hygiene and supervision guidance; it does not determine whether a
specific dog profile is compatible with a particular child or household.
