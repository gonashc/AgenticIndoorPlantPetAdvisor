# Pet food safety

CDC guidance notes that pet food and treats can be contaminated with germs. For a dog's or cat's
main diet, owners should look for labeling that indicates the food is complete and balanced. Someone
preparing food at home should consult a veterinarian or veterinary nutritionist to confirm that the
diet supplies appropriate nutrition. Food subject to a recall should not be fed, and product storage
instructions should be followed.

Hands should be washed before and after handling pet food or treats, with adults helping children
wash correctly. Bowls, scoops, placemats, and food-contact toys should be cleaned frequently. Pet
food should be kept away from human food preparation when practical, and a pet should not lick a
person's mouth, face, open wound, or broken skin immediately after eating. This is general food and
household safety guidance, not a diet recommendation or veterinary treatment plan.
