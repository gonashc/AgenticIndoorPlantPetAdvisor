# Current demo plant taxonomy

The USDA PLANTS checklist identifies four plants represented in this application. Spider plant is
listed under symbol CHCO28 as *Chlorophytum comosum* (Thunb.) Jacq., with the common name spider
plant and family Liliaceae. Parlor palm is listed under symbol CHEL5 as *Chamaedorea elegans* Mart.,
with the common name parlor palm and family Arecaceae. The checklist associates symbol EPPI and
synonym symbol EPAU with *Epipremnum aureum* (Lindl. & André) Bunting, the scientific name used for
pothos. Boston swordfern is listed under symbol NEEX as *Nephrolepis exaltata* (L.) Schott in the
family Dryopteridaceae; the application uses the common indoor name Boston fern.

These records support identity, spelling, and taxonomic matching only. They do not establish that a
plant is safe for dogs, cats, or children. They also do not determine indoor light, watering,
humidity, temperature, pest-management, or maintenance requirements. Those attributes remain
synthetic demo data until separately supported by an appropriate reviewed source.
