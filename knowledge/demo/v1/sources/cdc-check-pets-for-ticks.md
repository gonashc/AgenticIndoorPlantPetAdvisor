# Checking a pet for ticks

CDC advises checking pets for ticks every day, especially after they spend time outdoors. Run
fingers through the fur with gentle pressure to feel for small bumps. Areas highlighted for careful
inspection include in and around the ears, around the eyelids, under the collar, under the front
legs, between the back legs, between the toes, and around the tail.

Ticks can carry diseases that affect people and pets. A discovered tick should be removed as soon
as possible using appropriate guidance. Owners should ask a veterinarian about suitable prevention
for their individual pet. This record supports a general recurring inspection reminder. It does not
select a medication, diagnose disease, or establish a treatment schedule, and any product choice or
health concern should be handled with a veterinarian.
